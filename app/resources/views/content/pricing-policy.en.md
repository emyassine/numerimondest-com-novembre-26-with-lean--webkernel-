# Pricing & Engagement Policy

Our published prices make it easier to understand the level of engagement before a conversation. They are starting points for defined scopes; the final scope, schedule and fee are confirmed in a written proposal.

## Minimum build standards

Business website and CRM & client portal engagements start at **25,000 MAD**. Each is scoped around the organisation's goals, content, workflows and launch requirements. We do not reduce the work to a template-only handoff: the agreed proposal sets out the deliverables, responsibilities, review points and launch support before work begins.

## Clear, considered pricing

Fees use deliberate, rounded figures rather than retail-price endings. A listed starting price is not a promise that every project will fit that scope. Additional requirements, integrations, content or delivery constraints are discussed and priced in writing before approval.

## Payment and delivery

Fixed-scope projects are normally invoiced in two milestones: 50% to confirm the scope and reserve the delivery slot, and 50% at final review and handover. For engagements above 35,000 MAD, a 40% / 30% / 30% milestone schedule may be agreed in the proposal.

Monthly Revenue Desk engagements are billed monthly in advance. The 30-day pilot is a separate, one-time engagement intended to establish fit and working rhythm before an ongoing programme.

The Operations Audit fee is credited toward a qualifying Revenue Desk engagement started within 30 days, as stated in that service offer.

## Currency and written quotes

MAD is the catalogue's reference currency. Other currency figures are indicative conversions, refreshed from [Frankfurter API v2](https://frankfurter.dev/) reference rates where available, buffered by 2% for routine currency movement and rounded for readability. Their rate date is shown with the selected currency when available; reference rates are not a payment quote or a guarantee of a settlement rate.

Once we issue a written proposal, its fee is held for 14 calendar days. The proposal is the source of truth for the agreed currency, scope, payment schedule and validity period.

## A conversation before commitment

The shortlist and enquiry form help you share the services you are considering. They do not create an order or a payment obligation. Yassine and the account manager review the context with you, identify a suitable scope and explain the next step before any commitment.
