Pricing & engagement policy.

Our fee structures reflect direct principal involvement, senior-led execution, and the elimination of operational overhead for our clients. Every mandate is governed by written terms agreed prior to initiation.

1. Reference currency & multi-jurisdiction billing

MAD (Moroccan Dirham) serves as the baseline reference currency across our catalogue. For international mandates, written proposals may be denominated and settled in EUR or USD at the prevailing institutional exchange rate at the date of issuance.
